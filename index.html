12M — Facilities

Everything You Need for an Exceptional Diving Experience, All Within Reach.

At Azul, every facility is thoughtfully arranged to make your diving experience as comfortable and convenient as possible — from direct access to the sea and professional equipment assistance to dedicated training spaces and relaxation facilities.

  1. Direct Sea Access

    4 Stairways · 3 Underwater Platforms

    Enjoy direct access to the open sea through four dedicated stairways leading from the shoreline into deep water. Three submerged platforms provide designated areas for practical skills training and safety stops, supporting a smooth transition between training and underwater exploration.

  2. Underwater Museum

    Explore a different side of the underwater world with our dedicated seabed installation. Designed to enhance your diving experience beyond formal training, the underwater museum offers a distinctive setting for exploration, discovery, and underwater photography.

  3. Equipment Handling & Assistance

    From the moment you arrive, our team takes care of transporting your diving equipment from your vehicle to the waterfront and back again after your session. This convenient service allows you to focus on enjoying your dive without the additional effort of carrying your gear.

  4. Training Classrooms

    Our fully equipped classrooms provide a comfortable, organized environment for theoretical instruction and diving education. Designed to complement practical training, these devoted spaces allow divers to develop their knowledge and understanding before putting their skills into practice.

  5. Private Lockers & Secure Storage

    Enjoy greater peace of mind with private lockers available to both divers and instructors. Our designated storage facilities provide a convenient and secure place for personal belongings and equipment while you enjoy your time in the water.

  6. On-Site Accommodation

    Available Upon Request

    Make the most of your diving experience with the convenience of on-site accommodation. Whether you're planning an early morning dive or participating in a multi-day training course, overnight stays can be arranged in advance to accommodate your schedule.
